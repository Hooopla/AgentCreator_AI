**Concept Overview:**
EcoTech Couture is a high-fashion line that integrates smart textiles and sustainable materials to create garments that are not only stylish but also functional and eco-friendly. The aim is to redefine what it means to be fashionable in the modern world by combining aesthetics with technology and environmental consciousness.

**Key Features:**

1. **Smart Fabrics:**
   - Use of fabrics embedded with nanotechnology that can adjust temperature based on the wearer’s body heat, ensuring comfort in varying climates. Imagine a jacket that keeps you warm when it's cold outside and cools you down when you enter a heated room!

2. **Wearable Tech Integration:**
   - Incorporate small, lightweight devices that can track health metrics like heart rate, stress levels, and even air quality. These sensors can be discreetly woven into the fabric, making the garments not just clothes but a health monitoring system.

3. **Biodegradable Materials:**
   - Focus on sourcing materials that are biodegradable and made from recycled or organic sources. Think about using fabric derived from ocean plastics or hemp, which is both sustainable and stylish.

4. **Customizable Designs:**
   - Allow customers to personalize their garments through an accompanying app. They could choose colors, patterns, and even the tech features they want integrated, giving them a unique piece that reflects their individual style.

5. **Local Production:**
   - Partner with local artisans and small manufacturers to create limited-edition pieces, reducing carbon footprints and supporting local economies. Each piece could come with a story about the artisan who created it, adding a personal touch.

6. **Circular Fashion Approach:**
   - Implement a buy-back program where customers can return their old EcoTech Couture pieces in exchange for discounts on future purchases. The returned items would be refurbished or recycled into new garments.

**Target Audience:**
Fashion-forward, tech-savvy consumers who value sustainability and innovation. This line would appeal to young professionals, eco-conscious individuals, and anyone looking to make a statement with their wardrobe.

**Marketing Strategy:**
Utilize social media and influencer partnerships to showcase the garments in action—think videos of the smart features being demonstrated in various environments. Host pop-up events where customers can experience the technology firsthand, and incorporate augmented reality (AR) experiences that allow customers to visualize the customizations.

In a world where fashion is often at odds with sustainability, EcoTech Couture will turn heads while keeping the planet in mind. Are you ready to revolutionize the runway?
